# Report glossary

- New Glossary section, before Reproduce in both detail levels, defining every term the report
  uses: claim, source, parent and child, root, area, retired, claim level, integrity level (all
  four levels), verification method (all five), evidence, locator, evidence kind (all seven),
  stance, roll-up, each verdict marker, current verdict, rationale, auditor, finding, next step,
  gap, rigor gap, seal, and adopted record. Full mode adds the traceability matrix and evidence
  appendix.
- The Result introduction and the glossary's gap entry link to their sections (`@glossary`,
  `@gaps`).
- Integrity-level and verification-method meanings are defined once in the template and reused
  by the glossary, the Coverage introduction, and the rigor check.
- On `bdc.json` the summary grows from 41 to 42 pages.
