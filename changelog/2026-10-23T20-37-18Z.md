# Report text for readers outside claims-mcp

The PDF report is read by people auditing other projects, who may not know claims-mcp. The
rendered text now introduces the tool once and refers to nothing that exists only in the
claims-mcp repository.

- Result opens with a short paragraph explaining the claims ledger (claims, evidence, verdicts,
  derived claims) and linking to claims-mcp. Links are underlined.
- Integrity text says what a failed seal means without referring to "the server"; the
  not-checked note names `claims-mcp verify` instead of `just report`; adopted records are
  described as sealed during a ledger migration.
- Gap summary no longer cites "spec section 4.1"; the hierarchy intro no longer names the
  `derived_from` field.
- Reproduce explains that the command uses the claims-mcp CLI and its report template (with the
  template's path in the claims-mcp repository), drops the `cargo test` / `typst_template`
  instructions (they test claims-mcp, not the audited project) and the `just report` hint, and
  points readers to evidence locators to re-check verdicts.
- The default `method` on the cover is "claims-audit skill (claims-mcp)" instead of the
  repository path `skills/claims-audit`.
- The retired-claims note mentions the evidence appendix only in the full report.
