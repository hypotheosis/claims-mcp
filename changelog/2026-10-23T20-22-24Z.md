# Report review fixes: findings grouping and text helpers

- Findings never hide a refuted or not-enough-info claim. Only an area claim with active
  children heads a group; roots and childless areas (claims directly below a root with no
  children) are ordinary findings under "Top level". A group whose head is refuted or not
  enough info is kept even with no findings below it. "No active claim is refuted or
  undecided." appears only when there are no groups at all.
- A claim whose parent walk ends in a hand-edited cycle is treated as a root, so it is still
  listed when it is a finding.
- Text helpers (`report/text.typ`): `reason-key` accepts the Oxford comma (", and");
  `compress-runs` ends an id run at a word boundary; `wrappable` adds no wrap point inside
  `CLM-`/`EVD-` ids. Each has a new assertion in `report/text-tests.typ`.
- Reproduce section: the summary note names `just report full`, and the printed command writes
  `audit-full.pdf` for the full report.
- New template tests in `src/report.rs`: refuted heads without open children, grouping by the
  first active parent, and the `children of CLM-NNNN` roll-up locator.
- On `bdc.json`, CLM-0001 (a refuted root) now appears under Top level; the PDFs stay at 41
  pages (summary) and 796 pages (full).
- Spec: "Areas and findings grouping" in
  `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-report-detail-modes-design.md`.
