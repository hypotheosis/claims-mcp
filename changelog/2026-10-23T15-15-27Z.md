# claims-mcp 0.2.0: release

## Changed
- Version bumped to 0.2.0 in `Cargo.toml` (and `Cargo.lock`) and in `report/audit-notes.toml`.

## In this release (since 0.1.0)
- Integrity seals on the claims catalog (issue #9, PR #10):
  - The server refuses every write after a hand edit.
  - New `claims-mcp verify` and `claims-mcp seal --adopt` subcommands.
  - `schema_version` 2.
  - Integrity status in `audit_report` and the PDF report.
- Apache 2.0 license; `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` are now included in release archives (PR #6).
- Dependabot config and security policy (PR #7).

## Upgrading
- Catalogs written by 0.1.0 (`schema_version 1`) are read-only under 0.2.0 until a maintainer
  runs `claims-mcp --catalog claims.json seal --adopt` once and commits the result.
