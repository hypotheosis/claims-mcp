# Report: explain integrity levels and the rigor check

- Coverage by integrity level opens by defining integrity level (IEEE 1012: 4 safety,
  security, or data loss; 3 core function; 2 degraded function; 1 cosmetic or documentation).
- The rigor check states its rule (level 3-4 claims need test, analysis, demonstration, or
  formal proof, not inspection alone, with inspection defined) and then the result with counts:
  "All 713 level 3-4 claims meet this" or "RIGOR GAP: N of the M level 3-4 claims rely on
  inspection alone". A catalog with no level 3-4 claims says so.
- The `rigor` gap meaning reads "relies on inspection alone".
